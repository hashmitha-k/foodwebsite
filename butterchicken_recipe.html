Butter Chicken

Home Recipes Favorites Cooking Tips
    

Ingredients

  • 500g boneless chicken, cut into pieces
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1/4 cup heavy cream
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1/2 tsp cumin powder
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander for garnish
  • 1 tbsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)

Instructions

  1. Marinate Chicken: Mix chicken with ginger-garlic paste, chili powder, turmeric powder, and salt. Let it marinate for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Cook Chicken: Heat butter in a pan, and cook the chicken pieces until golden and cooked through. Set aside.
  3. Prepare Gravy: In the same pan, sauté onions until golden. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook until aromatic.
  4. Add pureed tomatoes, garam masala, cumin powder, and cook until oil separates from the masala.
  5. Add Chicken: Add the cooked chicken to the gravy, and mix well. Cook for 5 minutes.
  6. Finish Gravy: Add cream and kasuri methi, mix well, and cook for an additional 5 minutes.
  7. Serve: Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot with naan or rice.

Dietary Information

  • Calories: 400 kcal per serving
  • Protein: 25g
  • Fat: 30g
  • Carbohydrates: 15g
  • Non-Vegan: No
  • Gluten-Free: Yes
  • Servings: 4
