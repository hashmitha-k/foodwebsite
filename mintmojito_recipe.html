Mint Mojito

Ingredients

  • 10 fresh mint leaves
  • 1 tablespoon sugar (or to taste)
  • 1/2 lime, cut into wedges
  • 2 oz white rum
  • Club soda or sparkling water
  • Ice cubes
  • Lime slices (for garnish, optional)
  • Mint sprig (for garnish, optional)

Instructions

  1. Muddle the Mint: In a glass, muddle the mint leaves, lime wedges, and sugar.
  2. Add Rum & Ice: Pour in rum, add ice, and top with club soda.
  3. Garnish & Serve: Stir and garnish with lime slices and mint sprigs.
