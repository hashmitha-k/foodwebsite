Spaghetti Carbonara

Ingredients

  • 400g spaghetti
  • 150g pancetta or guanciale, diced
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/4 cup grated Pecorino Romano cheese
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • Olive oil, for cooking
  • Fresh parsley, chopped (for garnish)

Instructions

  1. Cook the Spaghetti: Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the spaghetti and cook until al dente, following package instructions. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water, then drain the pasta.
  2. Cook the Pancetta: While the pasta is cooking, heat a bit of olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Add the diced pancetta or guanciale and cook until crispy, about 5-7 minutes. Remove from heat and set aside.
  3. Prepare the Egg Mixture: In a bowl, whisk together the eggs, grated Parmesan, and Pecorino Romano cheese. Season with a pinch of salt and black pepper.
  4. Combine Everything: Once the pasta is drained, add it to the pan with the pancetta (still off the heat). Slowly pour the egg and cheese mixture over the pasta, tossing quickly to combine. Add reserved pasta water a little at a time until the sauce reaches your desired consistency.
  5. Serve: Serve the spaghetti carbonara immediately, garnished with fresh parsley and additional cheese if desired.

Diet Plan & Nutritional Information

Serving Size: 1 plate

  • Calories: 450 kcal
  • Fat: 20g
  • Carbohydrates: 50g
  • Protein: 18g
  • Sugar: 2g

Diet Tips:

  • For a lower-fat version: Use turkey bacon or a leaner cut of pancetta, and reduce the amount of cheese.
  • For a gluten-free version: Substitute gluten-free pasta.

Dietary Information

  • Gluten-Free: No (unless using gluten-free pasta)
  • Vegetarian: No (contains pancetta)
  • Contains Dairy: Yes
  • Contains Eggs: Yes
  • Vegan: No
